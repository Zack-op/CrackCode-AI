# CrackCode AI

CrackCode AI is an advanced conversational interview intelligence platform designed to simulate realistic technical and behavioral interviews.

Unlike traditional mock interview systems that simply read pre-generated questions, CrackCode AI creates an adaptive, memory-aware interviewer capable of:

- Understanding user responses
- Changing question difficulty dynamically
- Following conversational context
- Simulating realistic interview pressure
- Evaluating communication and technical depth
- Generating immersive interview experiences

The platform is evolving into a full AI-powered recruitment intelligence ecosystem.

---

# Vision

The goal of CrackCode AI is to create the closest possible simulation of a real interview environment.

Instead of:
- static question lists
- robotic voice agents
- disconnected conversations

CrackCode AI aims to build:

### A Living Interviewer

An interviewer that:
- listens
- adapts
- remembers
- challenges
- redirects
- probes deeper
- changes tone and difficulty
- evaluates communication behavior in real time

The system is designed to eventually simulate:
- FAANG-level technical interviews
- HR behavioral interviews
- pressure interviews
- startup founder interviews
- system design discussions
- AI engineering assessments
- real recruiter interaction patterns
